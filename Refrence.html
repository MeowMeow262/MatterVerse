PROJECT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
"MATTERVERSE is developed primarily based on the Myanmar Grade 10 Chemistry 1 Textbook, particularly Chapter 2 – Matter and Solutions.

For website planning and wireframing, Figma was used, while Visual Studio Code (VS Code) was used for coding and development. Graphic design and image editing were carried out using Adobe Photoshop, Canva, and Boxy SVG Editor.

Additional information, design inspiration, and technical guidance were gathered from Google Search, Pinterest, Dribbble, YouTube, W3Schools, Bootstrap Documentation, and other educational and development resources."
