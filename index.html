Learning Outcomes
After completing this chapter, students will be able to:
Explain ther theory of matter;
Identify the states based on the arrangement and movement of atoms and molecules;
Analyse the changes of state based on changes in the arrangement and movement of atoms and molecules and the level energy;
Discuss the charatistics of and the distinctions between elements, compounds and mixture;
Differentiate between physical and chemical changes;
Compare the characteristics of, behaviours of and connections between a solute, solvent and solution;
Solve the solubility of substances and the effect of temperature on it;
Describe separation techniques as applied to mixtures.
